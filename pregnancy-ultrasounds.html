2. Nuchal Translucency Scan (11 to 14 Weeks)

Warning Signs That Require an Urgent Pregnancy Scan

While routine scans follow a schedule, certain symptoms require an immediate pregnancy scan Ramanathapuram assessment. Do not wait for your next scheduled appointment if you experience any of the following:

  • Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy
  • Sudden reduction or absence of fetal movements after week 24
  • Severe abdominal cramping or pain
  • Signs of premature labour before 37 weeks
  • Leaking of fluid that may indicate membrane rupture
  • Severe headaches, visual disturbances, or sudden swelling (possible pre-eclampsia)
