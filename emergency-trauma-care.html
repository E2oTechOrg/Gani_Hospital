What to Do in a Medical Emergency near Ramanathapuram

Knowing how to respond in the first minutes of a medical emergency can save a life before you even reach the emergency ward Ramanathapuram. Follow these steps:

  • Stay calm — panic delays action. Focus on the patient
  • Call for help immediately — alert family, bystanders, or call Gani Hospital emergency
  • Do not move a trauma patient unless there is immediate danger — moving a spinal injury patient incorrectly can cause paralysis
  • Control visible bleeding — apply firm pressure with a clean cloth
  • Keep the patient breathing — check and clear the airway; do not give food or water
  • For suspected heart attack — have the patient sit or lie down calmly; do not leave them alone
  • For seizures — do not restrain; remove sharp objects; place in recovery position
