Safe Medicine Practices — What Our Pharmacists Want You to Know

A reliable medical shop near me does more than dispense medicines — it educates patients on safe use. Our duty pharmacists at Gani Hospitals pharmacy follow these patient safety principles at every counter interaction:

  • Never self-prescribe antibiotics: Antibiotic resistance is a growing threat in Tamil Nadu. Our prescription medicines Ramnad counter dispenses antibiotics only against valid prescriptions. Taking the wrong antibiotic for the wrong duration causes more harm than good.
  • Check expiry dates: Every medicine dispensed from our 24 hour pharmacy Ramanathapuram is checked for expiry before handing over. Never use medicines past their expiry date regardless of how they appear.
  • Store medicines correctly: Many medicines lose potency when stored in humid or hot conditions — a serious concern in coastal Ramanathapuram's climate. Our pharmacists advise on correct storage for every dispensed medicine.
  • Do not share prescription medicines: A medicine prescribed for one person's condition and body weight may be dangerous for another person, even with the same symptoms.
  • Dispose of unused medicines safely: Return unused or expired medicines to our Gani Hospitals pharmacy counter for safe disposal. Do not flush medicines down the drain or throw them in household waste.
