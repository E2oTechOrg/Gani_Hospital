Essential Prenatal Checkups: Tests & Scans You Should Not Skip

Updated April 2026 • 5 min read

Introduction

Prenatal checkups are essential for monitoring the health of both the mother and baby throughout pregnancy. These regular medical visits help track fetal development, identify risks early, and ensure a safe pregnancy journey.

From blood tests to ultrasound scans, each checkup plays a crucial role in preventing complications. Understanding these tests helps expecting mothers stay prepared, confident, and stress-free.

Why Prenatal Checkups Are Important

Regular prenatal checkups provide several benefits:

  • Monitor baby growth and development
  • Detect complications early
  • Manage maternal health conditions
  • Ensure safe pregnancy and delivery

First Trimester Tests (Weeks 1–12)

  • Blood Tests: Check blood group, anemia, infections
  • Urine Test: Detect sugar, protein, infections
  • Dating Ultrasound: Confirms pregnancy and due date
  • Thyroid Test: Ensures hormonal balance

Second Trimester Tests (Weeks 13–26)

  • Anomaly Scan: Detailed scan to check baby’s organs
  • Glucose Screening: Detects gestational diabetes
  • Quadruple Marker Test: Screens for genetic conditions

Third Trimester Tests (Weeks 27–40)

  • Growth Scan: Monitors baby size and position
  • Non-Stress Test (NST): Checks baby’s heart rate
  • Blood Pressure Monitoring: Detects preeclampsia

Common Prenatal Tests Explained

Some prenatal tests are routine, while others are recommended based on individual health conditions. Understanding them helps reduce anxiety and improves decision-making.

  • Hemoglobin Test: Detects anemia
  • Blood Sugar Test: Identifies gestational diabetes
  • Ultrasound Scans: Monitor baby growth and position
  • Genetic Screening: Detects inherited conditions

How Often Should You Visit the Doctor?

  • Every 4 weeks (early pregnancy)
  • Every 2 weeks (after 28 weeks)
  • Weekly visits (after 36 weeks)

Important Tips for Prenatal Care

  • Never skip scheduled checkups
  • Maintain a healthy diet
  • Take prescribed supplements
  • Report unusual symptoms immediately

Diet and Lifestyle During Pregnancy

A healthy lifestyle supports better outcomes during pregnancy. Along with regular checkups, focus on proper nutrition and daily habits.

  • Eat balanced meals rich in protein and vitamins
  • Stay hydrated throughout the day
  • Get enough rest and sleep
  • Avoid stress and heavy physical work

Frequently Asked Questions

How many prenatal visits are required?
Typically 8–10 visits are recommended for a healthy pregnancy.

Are ultrasound scans safe?
Yes, ultrasound scans are safe and commonly used to monitor baby development.

Can I skip tests if I feel healthy?
No, some conditions show no symptoms and can only be detected through tests.
