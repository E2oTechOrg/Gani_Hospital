Introduction – Understanding Diabetes

Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disease where blood sugar levels become abnormally high due to lack of insulin or insulin resistance.

If untreated, it may lead to serious complications like: heart disease, kidney failure, nerve damage, and vision loss.

That is why early consultation with a sugar doctor near me is essential.

Types of Diabetes

  • Type 1 Diabetes: No insulin production
  • Type 2 Diabetes: Lifestyle-related (most common)
  • Gestational Diabetes: Occurs during pregnancy

Symptoms of Diabetes (Early Warning Signs)

  • Frequent urination
  • Excess thirst
  • Sudden weight loss
  • Fatigue
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow wound healing

Complications of Uncontrolled Diabetes

If diabetes is not managed properly, it can lead to serious long-term health complications affecting multiple organs of the body.

  • Heart disease and stroke risk
  • Kidney failure (diabetic nephropathy)
  • Nerve damage (neuropathy)
  • Eye damage and vision loss (retinopathy)
  • Foot ulcers and infections
