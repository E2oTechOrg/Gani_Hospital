Trusted Healthcare Provider

Gani Hospitals is a leading multi-speciality hospital in Ramanathapuram, trusted by families for quality healthcare, experienced doctors, and patient-friendly treatment. With modern facilities and compassionate care, we focus on providing safe and effective medical services for every patient.
