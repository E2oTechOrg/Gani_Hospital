Child Vaccination Schedule (Birth to 5 Years)

A complete, doctor-approved guide to child vaccination schedule in India covering essential vaccines, benefits, safety, and expert pediatric care.

Introduction

Vaccination is one of the most important steps in protecting your child from life-threatening diseases. A proper child vaccination schedule ensures immunity is built at the right time during early childhood.

At Gani Hospital, we follow safe, government-approved immunization schedules recommended by pediatric experts.

From birth to 5 years, vaccines protect children from infections like polio, hepatitis, measles, tuberculosis, diphtheria, and more.

Why Vaccination is Important

  • Protects from deadly childhood diseases
  • Builds strong immune system
  • Prevents outbreaks in community
  • Reduces hospitalization risk
  • Supports healthy growth & development

Complete Vaccination Schedule (0–5 Years)

At Birth

  • BCG – Tuberculosis protection
  • OPV – Polio prevention
  • Hepatitis B – Liver infection protection

6 Weeks

  • DTP – Diphtheria, Tetanus, Pertussis
  • IPV – Polio protection
  • Hib – Brain infection prevention
  • Rotavirus – Diarrhea protection

10–14 Weeks

  • Booster doses of DTP, IPV, Hib

6 Months

  • Hepatitis B completion
  • Influenza vaccine

9–12 Months

  • MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella)

1–2 Years

  • Chickenpox vaccine
  • Hepatitis A
  • DTP booster

4–5 Years

  • Final booster doses (DTP & MMR)

Benefits of Timely Vaccination

Following the correct vaccination schedule ensures long-term protection and reduces risk of serious illness.

  • Strong immunity development
  • Protection from infections early in life
  • Better school health readiness
  • Reduced future medical complications

Side Effects (Normal & Temporary)

  • Mild fever
  • Swelling at injection site
  • Irritability
  • Reduced appetite

These effects usually disappear within 24–48 hours.

When to See a Doctor

  • High fever above 102°F
  • Severe allergic reaction
  • Continuous crying
  • Breathing difficulty
