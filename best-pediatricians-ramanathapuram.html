Warning Signs - When Should Your Child See a Pediatrician?

Many parents hesitate before visiting a doctor, wondering if a symptom is serious enough. With children, consulting early is always the right choice. Visit our best pediatrician in Ramanathapuram if your child shows any of these signs:

  • High or persistent fever above 38.5 degrees for more than two days
  • Continuous cough, wheezing, or breathing difficulty
  • Poor feeding, refusal to drink fluids, or signs of dehydration
  • Repeated vomiting or loose stools lasting more than one day
  • Skin rashes, hives, or unexplained swelling
  • Noticeable weight loss or poor height gain over time
  • Repeated ear, throat, or urinary infections
  • Unusual drowsiness, irritability, or very low energy
  • Sleep disturbances or bedwetting beyond typical age
  • Delay in reaching speech, walking, or social milestones
