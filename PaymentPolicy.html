Payment Policy

A detailed invoice is issued at the time of discharge or after each outpatient visit. The invoice includes all charges for consultations, investigations, medicines, procedures, and room rent.

Gani Hospital provides healthcare services including consultations, diagnostics, treatments, and surgeries. All services are subject to availability and physician discretion.

Appointments can be booked online, via phone, or in person. Walk-in patients are accommodated subject to availability. We reserve the right to reschedule appointments due to emergencies or unavailability of the physician.

We reserve the right to update these terms at any time. Changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of our services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
